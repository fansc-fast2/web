## 1. 只讲清一件事

fast2x 正在为有实际产品、有海外官网建设与运营需要的企业，开发从网站建设到持续运行的基础能力。官网主语言英语，目标市场美国，域名 fast2x.com。

内容主线：**Who it is for → What we’re building → How it fits together → Where we are today**。

首屏先讲业务方向，不以 AI、SEO/GEO、Shopify 或 Strapi 定义整个品牌。所有能力都在开发中，当前官网只承担初始化宣传。

## 3. 首页 5 个 block 与英文文案

以下为可供排版的英文初稿。设计备注为内部说明，不放入官网。预计正文约 300–400 个英文词，桌面约 3–4 屏，以实际排版为准；不把每个 block 做成全屏。

### H01 · Hero · `#top`

**任务：** 一句话建立业务范围，明确当前阶段。

**Eyebrow:** In development

**H1:** Your product website. From first page to ongoing operations.

**Body:** fast2x is developing a foundation for businesses that need to build and run a product website for international audiences. We’re bringing website creation, content management and the foundations for ongoing operations into one connected approach.

**CTA:** Explore the Vision → `/#vision`

**设计备注：** 文案优先，使用品牌 tokens；可配一张标注 Conceptual overview 的简单结构图。不放产品后台假截图、指标或客户标识。

### H02 · Audience · `#who`

**任务：** 让访客判断是否与自己相关，避免另开行业/客户页。

**H2:** For businesses with products to share—and a website to run.

**Body:** You have products to introduce and customers to reach. Your website needs to explain what you offer, keep information current and support the work that continues after launch.

**Supporting line:** Our initial focus is businesses with international website needs, with the United States as our first target market.

**设计备注：** 紧凑文字区，不拆客户类型卡片，不虚构行业限制。

### H03 · Planned Scope · `#vision`

**任务：** 用三件容易理解的事替代原来的产品/能力目录。

**H2:** What we’re building.

**Body:** Three connected areas, all in development.

| Card heading | Body |
| --- | --- |
| Build your website | A foundation for creating a clear, well-structured website around your products and brand. |
| Manage your content | A planned way to organize product information and maintain the pages that explain your business. |
| Support ongoing operations | An operating foundation designed around the work of keeping a website running and improving over time. |

**设计备注：** 仅 3 张卡片，均标 In development。无独立详情链接。不细分 SEO、AI Agent、产品知识库、多站点或安全能力。

### H04 · Approach · `#approach`

**任务：** 解释三部分为什么放在一起，而不是宣传已经可执行的产品工作流。

**H2:** Think beyond launch day.

**Body:** Building a website is the beginning. Our direction is to connect the decisions made before launch with the content and operating needs that follow.

| Step | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Start with your products | Shape the website around what your business offers and what visitors need to understand. |
| Plan for change | Consider how product information and website content will be maintained as the business evolves. |
| Build for continuity | Keep ongoing operation in view from the start, alongside the design and construction of the site. |

**设计备注：** 3 个静态步骤；标注 Planned approach。不是已上线系统流程，不添加连接账户、运行扫描或执行任务按钮。

### H05 · Current Status · `#status`

**任务：** 结束时清楚交代状态，避免访客寻找尚未开放的服务入口。

**H2:** Introducing the direction. Building the foundation.

**Body:** fast2x is in development. This site introduces what we’re working toward. All capabilities are being developed, and public demos are not available at this stage.

**Closing line:** Website creation. Content management. Ongoing operations.

**设计备注：** 简短收尾，无表单、订阅、预约或上线时间承诺。状态说明同时出现在首屏，不要求访客滚动到底部才得知。

### Shared Header / Footer

**Brand:** fast2x

**Header links:** Vision / Approach / Status

**Footer description:** A foundation for building and running product websites.

**Footer status:** In development

**Domain:** fast2x.com

页脚不增加公司地址、邮箱、法律主体、社交账号或未经确认的版权归属。
